P09 · SMART FARM INPUT MANAGEMENT

From scattered farm inputs to traceable field actions.

Problem Statement P09 asks for inventory, field/crop mapping, usage records, reminders, suppliers and reports. AgriNova puts those workflows behind simple farmer-friendly buttons, with browser-local data for the prototype.

📦 Input inventory

Track seeds, fertilizer, pesticides and equipment quantities, minimum stock and suppliers.

🗺️ Field & crop mapping

Connect saved field locations to the crop being grown there.

🧾 Usage traceability

Record what input was used, where, when, why and how much, while updating stock.

⏰ Reminders

Plan refill, application, inspection and equipment-maintenance dates.

🤝 Suppliers

Keep supplier contacts and the input categories they provide in one place.

📊 Reports

Review stock, low-stock items, usage history and export a simple CSV report.

Offline-ready prototype: input, usage, reminder and supplier data are stored locally in the browser so the core workflow remains usable when connectivity is poor.

HOW IT WORKS

Four steps from field data to action.

1
Collect

Enter crop, location, area, soil and field conditions.

2
Analyze

The prototype applies transparent decision rules to the entered values.

3
Interpret

The system converts measurements into health, irrigation, risk and yield indicators.

4
Act

The dashboard gives a simple recommendation that can guide the next field check.

AI INTELLIGENCE

What the analysis engine checks.

This prototype keeps the P09 decision logic transparent; AI is a support layer, not a replacement for verified agricultural advice.

Crop Health Score

Combines moisture and weather conditions into a demo health indicator.

Irrigation Decision

Low moisture raises an irrigation recommendation; adequate moisture reduces urgency.

Risk Detection

Very low moisture or high weather risk produces a visible warning.

Yield Estimate

Uses a transparent demo formula based on area and crop-health conditions.

Action Message

Turns the analysis into one concise recommendation for the farmer.

Future ML Layer

A later version can replace the rule engine with a model trained on real datasets.
